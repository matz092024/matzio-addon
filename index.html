Welcome to Matzio

Your own streaming addon for Stremio, running quietly on this PC. Setup takes about two minutes: connect your debrid service, check your internet speed, choose how streams are sorted, and install.

Connect your debrid service

Matzio checks which streams are already cached by your debrid service, so they play instantly. Add one or both.

How fast is your internet?

Matzio uses this to skip streams too big to play smoothly. A 4K film needs far more than a 1080p episode, so it compares each file's size with its runtime.

What matters most to you?

Drag to rank. Streams are compared on the top item first, and the next ones break ties. You can change this any time.

Add Matzio to Stremio

Stremio opens and asks you to confirm. You only do this once; Matzio keeps working whenever it's in your tray.

Debrid

Accounts Matzio uses to find cached streams and play them instantly.

Sources

Where Matzio looks for streams. All switched-on sources are searched at once and duplicates are merged; a source that is down or slow is simply skipped.

Sorting

Drag to rank what matters most. Streams are compared on #1 first, then #2 to break ties, and so on.

Filters

Every check can be switched off on its own, and can either hide streams or just move them to the bottom. Preferences (★) rank streams through their place under Sorting.

Subtitles

Subtitles from OpenSubtitles and SubDL, listed in Stremio's player next to the ones Stremio finds. Each provider needs your own free key, which stays on this PC.

Stream display

How each stream is labelled in Stremio's picker.
